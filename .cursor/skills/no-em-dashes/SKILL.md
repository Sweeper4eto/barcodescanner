# No em dashes in text

## Rule

**Never** put an em dash `—` (U+2014) in text you write or edit.

En dash `–` (U+2013) is **OK for ranges** (dates, times, numbers, weekday spans).
Do **not** use en dash as a sentence break.

## Use instead of em dash

- Period / new sentence
- Comma
- Colon
- Parentheses
- Regular hyphen `-` for compounds (`well-known`) and placeholders (`- no client -`)

## Ranges (keep en dash)

Good: `7:00–22:00`, `3–20`, `Mon–Sun`, `пон–нед`, `{start}–{end}`, `25.09.2026`

## Examples

Bad: `Rearrange icons the way you work — move tiles and switch pages.`  
Good: `Rearrange icons the way you work. Move tiles and switch pages.`

Bad: `Waste, markdowns, and staff activity — see what is happening.`  
Good: `Waste, markdowns, and staff activity. See what is happening in the store.`

Bad range rewrite: `7:00-22:00` when the product already uses `7:00–22:00`  
Good: leave `7:00–22:00` as en dash

## When editing existing copy

If you touch a string that already contains `—` or a prose sentence-break `–`, rewrite that string to remove the break in the same change. Leave range en dashes alone.
